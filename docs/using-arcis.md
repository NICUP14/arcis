# How to use Arcis

Everything happens on the phone, without looking at it: it's a trackpad for the big
screen in your glasses. The **Gestures** tile in Arcis Home shows all of this in the
glasses too.

## The phone screen

While the glasses are connected, the phone shows a black trackpad:

```
 ┌──────────────────────┐
 │ ▯                    │  ← upright / sideways switch (top corner)
 │                      │
 │                    │ │  ← scroll strip along the right edge
 │     trackpad       │ │
 │                    │ │
 │ ▶ Media   [⌨] [🎤] │ │  ← video chip (when a video plays), keyboard, mic
 │ ───────────────────  │  ← bottom edge: switch apps, Home
 └──────────────────────┘
```

- **Upright or sideways.** The button in the top corner switches between holding the
  phone upright (one hand) and sideways (two hands). The layout stays put until you
  switch: no surprise rotations.
- **Left-handed?** *Settings › Hand › Left* mirrors everything: the scroll strip moves
  to the left edge and the buttons to the bottom left.

## Pointer and clicks

| On the phone | Does |
|---|---|
| Slide one finger | Move the pointer (faster swipes go further) |
| Tap | Click |
| Hold still, then lift | Right-click (on a phone app: long-press) |
| Tap, then tap-and-hold and move | Drag (select text, move things) |
| Two fingers up / down, or one finger along the right edge | Scroll |
| Pinch two fingers apart / together | Zoom in / out |
| Tap with two fingers | Back (on a PC: Esc) |

Pointer and scroll speed can be changed in *Settings*.

## Switching apps

Everything you open (phone apps, your PC, a Bluetooth device) is in one list, like the
recent apps on a phone. All of it happens on the **bottom edge** of the phone:

| On the phone | Does |
|---|---|
| Swipe **right** along the bottom | Previous app |
| Swipe **left** along the bottom | Next app |
| Swipe **up** from the bottom | Arcis Home |
| Swipe **up from the bottom and hold** | All open apps as cards: slide to choose, let go to open, flick up to close one |

The name of the app you switched to flashes briefly in the glasses.

**Your PC keeps its place.** Moonlight (the app that shows your PC) ends its stream when
another app covers it; that's how Moonlight works. Arcis brings it back: your PC shows
up in the list as **"My PC ⟳"**, and switching to it, or pressing Back from another
app, reconnects in a second or two. Your PC's programs keep running the whole time.

**Unplugged by accident?** If the glasses come back within 10 minutes, whatever was on
screen reopens by itself.

## Typing and speaking

- **Keyboard button** (bottom corner): opens your phone's own keyboard on the phone, with
  its autocorrect and swipe typing. Point at a text field in the glasses and click it
  first, or just start typing when your PC or a Bluetooth device is in front. Tap the
  button again to close the keyboard.
- **Mic button**: **hold** it, speak, let go. Your words appear in a strip in the glasses
  while you speak, and are typed when you let go. Change the language in *Settings ›
  Dictation language*.
- The volume buttons stay plain volume buttons.

## Drawn shortcuts

**Rest your finger on the phone until it ticks** (a short vibration), then draw a shape
in one stroke. The shapes look like the key or the letter of the shortcut:

| Draw | Does | | Draw | Does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<` | Backspace | | `C` | Copy |
| `>` | Tab | | `V` | Paste |
| `↵` (down, then left) | Enter | | `\` | Cut |
| `⊔` (a cup) | Space | | `Z` | Undo |
| `D` | Delete | | `Z` drawn backwards | Redo |
| a straight line | Arrow key that way | | `∧` (a peak) | Select all |
| `7` | Previous app (like Alt+Tab) | | `S` | Save |
| `W` | Home (Start menu on a PC) | | a magnifier (circle + handle) | Find |

On a PC they become the usual shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V…, Cmd on a Mac). The Gestures
card in the glasses shows each stroke with a dot where to start.

## Watching videos (media mode)

When the app in the glasses is playing a video (YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video…), the
trackpad becomes a remote, no aiming needed:

| On the phone | Does |
|---|---|
| Tap | Play / pause |
| Swipe left / right | Skip back / forward 10 s (longer swipes skip more) |
| Slide along the scroll strip (right edge, or left if left-handed) | Volume |
| Hold still, then lift | Get the pointer back |

A **▶ Media** chip on the phone shows that media mode is on; tap it to turn it off or on.
For your **PC** media mode starts off; tap **▶ Media off** to control a video playing
on the PC (play/pause, arrow keys to skip, volume).

Needs *Notification access* (setup checklist), because that's how Android lets an app
see what is playing.

## Message banners

Messages and calls pop up at the top of the glasses for 4 seconds: app icon, sender
and the first line.

- **Tap with two fingers** while it's showing to open it in the glasses.
- **Swipe up** to dismiss it.

Silent and ongoing notifications (music, downloads) are skipped. In *Settings ›
Message banners* you can turn them off, or show them only while your PC or a video is
on screen. Notification text stays on the phone: it is never sent to your PC.

## The status bar

Over apps in the glasses, the top-right corner shows the **time**, the **phone's
battery**, your **PC** (green dot when connected), a **red dot** while the microphone is
on, and **▶ Media** in media mode. It hides while your PC fills the screen (swipe along
the bottom edge to see it for 3 s). Turn it off in *Settings › Status bar*.

## Controlling another device over Bluetooth

Arcis Home › **Bluetooth** turns the phone into a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard for a
laptop, tablet or TV. You won't see that device in the glasses, only a "controlling"
screen; look at its own screen.

1. The first time: **Pair a new device** makes the phone visible for 2 minutes. On the
   other device, open its Bluetooth settings and pair with your phone.
2. Then pick the device in the list. The trackpad moves its pointer; the keyboard and
   mic buttons type on it.
3. To leave, swipe up from the bottom edge.

## Settings

Open **Settings** in Arcis Home (in the glasses):

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Pointer speed / Scroll speed | How far a swipe moves the pointer / scrolls |
| Dictation language | The language you speak with the mic button |
| Hand | Right or left: which side the scroll strip and buttons are on |
| Status bar | The time / battery bar in the corner of the glasses |
| Message banners | On / off, always or only while streaming |
| Phone setup | Opens the setup checklist on the phone again |
